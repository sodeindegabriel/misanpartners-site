Privacy

Misan Partners investor portal

What we collect

When you are invited to the investor portal, we collect and keep only what is needed to run it:

  • Your email address, used to invite you and sign you in.
  • Access requests you make for documents, including which file you asked for and the decision we made.
  • Portal activity, such as when you last signed in.

Google Drive

Portal documents are stored in Google Drive. The portal accesses Drive read-only, and solely to show documents to invited investors. It does not create, edit or delete anything in Drive, and does not read your own Google account.

No selling or sharing

We never sell your data and we do not share it with third parties for their own use.

Where data is stored

Account and access-request data is stored in Supabase. The portal itself is hosted on Vercel. Both act as service providers that process data on our behalf.

Contact

Questions about your data, or want it removed? Email c@misanpartners.com.
